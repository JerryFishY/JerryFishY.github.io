Ray Yu

I am a researcher at Peking University interested in secure and efficient computing. My work focuses on making privacy-preserving computation practical through homomorphic encryption, high-performance systems, and hardware acceleration.

I enjoy working across abstraction layers, from cryptographic primitives and numerical methods to GPU kernels and specialized architectures. I am also interested in efficient machine learning systems and encrypted inference.
